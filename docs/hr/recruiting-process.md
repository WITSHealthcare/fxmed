# FXMed Recruiting Process

This is how FXMed takes a candidate from application to first day: the pipeline stages and their time limits, the interview loop for each role family, the scorecards the panel fills in, the reference and credential checks, how offers are approved, and how long we keep candidate data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. It replaces "apply by email" with a tracked, fair and repeatable process that a small Lagos clinic can actually run. Roles and bands come from `docs/hr/hiring-plan.md` and `docs/hr/job-descriptions.md`; new hires then follow `docs/new-hire-training-plan.md`.

Owner: Head of Talent Acquisition (to assign). Day-to-day operator: HR and Admin Officer once hired (R07).

---

## 1. Who does what

| Role in the process | Who | Does |
|---|---|---|
| Requester | Hiring manager | Raises the requisition with trigger evidence; writes the scorecard must-haves |
| Approver | Managing Director (all roles); Medical Director (clinical roles) | Approves the requisition and the offer |
| Recruiter | Head of Talent Acquisition / HR Officer | Adverts, screening, scheduling, checks, offer letter, candidate communication |
| Panel | 2–4 interviewers per role (see Section 3) | Interview and score independently |
| Debrief chair | Hiring manager | Runs the debrief; records the decision and reasons |

No one interviews alone for a final decision, and no one scores a candidate they are related to or have a close personal relationship with. Declare conflicts at scheduling.

## 2. Pipeline stages

Each candidate is in exactly one stage. A candidate can move to **Rejected** or **Withdrawn** from any stage; the reason is recorded.

| # | Stage | What happens | Owner | Time limit (working days) | Exit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Requisition approved | Role, band, panel and scorecard agreed; opening published on the Careers page | Hiring manager, Approver | — | Opening published |
| 1 | Applied | Candidate submits the application form with CV and privacy notice acknowledged | Candidate | — | Auto-acknowledged within 1 day |
| 2 | Screened | Recruiter checks must-haves (qualification, licence, years, location) against the JD | Recruiter | 3 | Pass → 3; fail → Rejected (template email) |
| 3 | Phone screen | 20–30 min call: motivation, availability, salary expectation vs band, notice period, right to work | Recruiter | 5 | Pass → 4 |
| 4 | Assessment | Role-specific task or practical (Section 3) | Recruiter, hiring manager | 5 | Scored → 5 |
| 5 | Interview loop | Panel interviews; each interviewer submits a scorecard before the debrief | Panel | 7 | → 6 |
| 6 | Debrief and decision | Panel meets; hiring manager records hire / no hire with reasons | Hiring manager | 2 | Hire → 7 |
| 7 | References and credentials | Two references, licence verification, certificates, identity (Section 5) | Recruiter | 7 | All clear → 8 |
| 8 | Offer approval | Offer form approved per Section 6 | Approver | 2 | Approved → 9 |
| 9 | Offer extended | Written offer sent; verbal call first | Recruiter | — | Accept / decline within 5 days |
| 10 | Accepted | Contract signed; start date fixed; pre-employment medical booked | Recruiter | — | → 11 |
| 11 | Pre-boarding | Portal account invited with the correct role; training programmes assigned; buddy and supervisor named; equipment ready | HR Officer, Admin | Before day 1 | → Hired |
| 12 | Hired | Candidate record becomes an employee record; application data moves to the employee file | HR Officer | Day 1 | Closed |

**Target time to hire:** 30 working days from opening to accepted offer for most roles; 45 for R09 Medical Officer and R06 Pharmacist.

**Candidate communication standard:** every candidate hears from us within 5 working days at every stage, including a clear "no". Rejection after an interview is by phone or a personal email, not a template.

## 3. Interview loops by role family

Each family has a fixed loop so that candidates for the same role get the same questions and tasks. Interviews can be in person (Lagos) or video; practicals for clinical roles are in person.

### Family A — Physicians (R09 Medical Officer, L01 Locums)

| Step | Format | Panel | Length | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Case review: candidate is given an anonymised patient with history and lab results the day before, and presents an assessment and plan | Medical Director + one clinician | 45 min | Clinical reasoning; use of FM ranges without over-calling; when to refer |
| A2 | Structured interview | Medical Director + Managing Director | 45 min | Patient communication, ethics, teamwork, motivation for FM |
| A3 | Simulated consultation (role-play with a staff "patient", e.g. anxious parent, sceptical patient) | Clinician + Patient Care or Sales staff member | 30 min | Explaining, listening, no cure claims |
| A4 (locums) | A1 and A3 only, then a paid trial session observed | Medical Director | 1 session | Real-world practice |

### Family B — Nursing, midwifery and lab (R01, R02, R12)

| Step | Format | Panel | Length | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Practical: skills station (IV cannulation on a training arm, sample labelling and cold-chain set-up, or newborn check on a manikin) | Senior nurse or MLS + Medical Director | 30 min | Technique, infection control, documentation |
| B2 | Scenario questions: home-visit situations (patient deteriorates, family member asks for results, unsafe home) | Medical Director + Clinical Operations Coordinator | 40 min | Judgement, escalation, safeguarding, confidentiality |
| B3 | Values interview | Managing Director or Head of Talent | 30 min | Reliability, patient warmth, fit |

### Family C — Allied health and pharmacy (R05 Dietitian, R06 Pharmacist, R10 SLT)

| Step | Format | Panel | Length | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Take-home or on-site task: Dietitian builds a 3-day Nigerian meal plan for a Gut Repair case; Pharmacist reviews a supplement list for interactions with a patient's medicines; SLT outlines goals from a case summary | Medical Director + relevant professional (external if needed) | 2 hrs prep, 30 min discussion | Professional competence |
| C2 | Structured interview | Medical Director + Managing Director | 45 min | Communication, scope of practice, collaboration |
| C3 (Pharmacist only) | Compliance discussion: storage, recalls, NAFDAC status, premises obligations | Managing Director + counsel if available | 30 min | Regulatory knowledge |

### Family D — Sales, marketing and patient care (R04, R11, R13)

| Step | Format | Panel | Length | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Role-play: respond to a real-style lead (health assessment result, Zara handover, price objection) by call and by written message | Sales lead + clinician | 30 min | Listening, honesty, knowing what to hand to clinical |
| D2 | Written task: CRM entry and follow-up plan for 5 leads (R04/R13), or a blog outline and campaign plan (R11) | Sales lead | 1 hr | Organisation, writing |
| D3 | Structured interview | Managing Director + Patient Care or Ops staff | 30 min | Values, resilience, fit |

### Family E — Operations, HR and finance (R03, R07, R08)

| Step | Format | Panel | Length | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Work sample: R03 plans a day of 8 home visits across Lagos with a lab pick-up; R07 drafts an onboarding checklist and spots errors in a sample payroll input; R08 reconciles a month of sample receipts and computes pension deductions | Hiring manager | 1 hr | Accuracy, judgement |
| E2 | Structured interview | Managing Director + one clinician (R03) or the external accountant (R08) | 45 min | Ownership, confidentiality, process thinking |

### Family F — Technology (T01)

| Step | Format | Panel | Length | What it tests |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | Code review of a small pull request containing a data-access flaw (e.g. a route that skips the admin check) | Managing Director + external technical reviewer | 45 min | Security instinct, code quality |
| F2 | Paid take-home: small feature with a migration and an admin API route | External reviewer | 4 hrs, paid | Delivery |
| F3 | Structured interview | Managing Director | 30 min | Communication, reliability |

## 4. Scorecards

Every interviewer fills in a scorecard **before** the debrief and without seeing others' scores. Each step in Section 3 maps to named competencies; interviewers score only the competencies their step covers and write the evidence (what the candidate said or did).

### Rating scale

| Rating | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does not meet | Clear gaps or concerns; would need more than normal training to be safe in the role |
| 2 | Partly meets | Some evidence, but inconsistent or shallow; would need close support |
| 3 | Meets | Solid evidence at the level the role needs on day one |
| 4 | Exceeds | Strong, specific evidence above the level the role needs; could teach others |

### Core competencies (every role)

| Competency | 1 — Does not meet | 2 — Partly meets | 3 — Meets | 4 — Exceeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Patient-centred communication | Talks over the patient; jargon; dismissive | Polite but generic; misses the patient's main worry | Listens, checks understanding, explains next step plainly | Adapts to each person (anxious parent, sceptic, elderly); builds trust quickly |
| Integrity and scope | Would make cure claims, overstep scope or share data casually | Knows the rules but hesitates when pressured | States limits clearly; escalates correctly; no over-claiming | Spots grey areas others miss and handles them well |
| Confidentiality and data care | Suggests sending records via personal WhatsApp or similar | Aware of privacy but vague on practice | Describes correct day-to-day handling (work channels, minimum data, locked devices) | Gives examples of improving privacy practice in past roles |
| Reliability and ownership | Vague on past commitments; blames others | Reliable when supervised | Concrete examples of finishing what they start, on time | Examples of fixing problems no one assigned to them |
| Teamwork | Prefers to work alone; poor handovers | Cooperates but handovers incomplete | Clear handovers; asks for and gives help | Makes the team around them better |
| Learning and FM fit | Dismissive of functional medicine or of evidence | Curious but unclear what FM means | Understands FM as root-cause and evidence-based, working with conventional care | Already learning in the field; balanced view of evidence |

### Role-family competencies

| Family | Competency | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Physicians | Clinical reasoning | Unsafe or missing differentials | Reasonable but misses key findings | Sound assessment and plan; appropriate tests | Elegant, prioritised plan; anticipates follow-up |
| A Physicians | Use of FM ranges | Over-calls disease from optimal ranges | Mixes optimal and diagnostic ranges | Distinguishes optimal vs standard; explains to patient | Teaches the distinction; knows the evidence limits |
| A Physicians | Referral judgement | Misses red flags | Refers late or vaguely | Refers at the right time with a clear letter | Builds referral relationships |
| B Nursing/lab | Technical skill | Unsafe technique | Correct but slow or unsure | Safe, clean, efficient | Excellent, including in children |
| B Nursing/lab | Infection control and sample integrity | Breaks asepsis or labelling rules | Minor lapses | No lapses; explains why | Improves the SOP |
| B Nursing/lab | Home-visit judgement | Unsafe response to scenario | Right answer after prompting | Right, prompt escalation | Handles family dynamics and safety calmly |
| C Allied/pharmacy | Professional competence (task) | Errors that could harm | Generic, not tailored | Correct, tailored to the case and Nigerian context | Insightful; adds what we did not ask for |
| C Allied/pharmacy | Regulatory awareness (pharmacist) | Unaware of obligations | Partial | Knows storage, recalls, NAFDAC and premises duties | Has set up compliance before |
| D Sales/patient care | Lead handling | Pushy or loses the lead | Gets to a booking but misses qualification | Qualifies, books, hands over cleanly | Converts while building trust; perfect notes |
| D Sales/patient care | Written communication | Errors; unclear | Clear but cold or long | Clear, warm, short | Messages patients would forward |
| E Ops/HR/finance | Accuracy | Multiple errors in work sample | One material error | No material errors | Finds errors in our sample |
| E Ops/HR/finance | Planning and prioritisation | No plan | Plan ignores constraints (traffic, cut-offs) | Workable plan with contingencies | Better plan than ours |
| F Technology | Security and data protection | Misses the flaw | Finds it after hints | Finds and fixes it | Finds related issues and proposes guards |
| F Technology | Code quality | Hard to read; untested | Works but messy | Clean, matches repo patterns | Simplifies the surrounding code |

### Decision rules

- Any **1** on Integrity and scope, Confidentiality, Clinical reasoning, Technical skill (B), or Security (F) means **no hire**, whatever the other scores.
- Hire requires an average of **3.0 or more** across all scored competencies and no more than one **2**.
- A split panel is settled by the hiring manager with the reason written down; the Approver can overrule only in writing.
- Scores and notes are kept with the candidate record (see retention, Section 8).

## 5. References and credential checks

Done after the decision to hire and before the offer is approved. The candidate consents in the application form and again before references are contacted.

### References

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Number | Two professional referees; one must be a recent line manager or clinical supervisor |
| Method | Phone call using the standard script, recorded on the reference form; written reference accepted as a supplement |
| Questions | Dates and role; reliability; clinical or work quality; conduct and patient complaints; would they re-employ |
| Current employer | Contacted only with the candidate's permission, usually after a conditional offer |
| Guarantors | If FXMed asks for a guarantor (common in Lagos for cash-handling roles), collect only name, contact and relationship, and keep it with the employee file (confirm with counsel whether a guarantor is appropriate) |

### Credentials by role

| Check | Roles | How | Recorded |
|---|---|---|---|
| Identity (NIN) | All | Sight NIN slip or card; verify through an approved channel | NIN last digits, verified date, verifier |
| Right to work | All non-Nigerians | Valid residence and work permit / expatriate quota position (confirm with counsel) | Document type, expiry |
| Academic qualification | All with degree requirement | Sight original certificate; verify with institution for clinical roles | Qualification, institution, year, verified |
| MDCN registration and annual practising licence | R09, L01 | Check MDCN register / verification portal; sight current licence | Licence number, expiry date |
| NMCN registration and licence (RN, RM) | R01, R12 | Check NMCN verification; sight current licence | Licence number(s), expiry |
| MLSCN registration and annual licence | R02 | Check MLSCN; sight current licence | Licence number, expiry |
| PCN registration and annual licence | R06 | Check PCN register; sight current licence | Licence number, expiry |
| Dietitians' council registration | R05 | Check council register (confirm body with counsel) | Number, expiry |
| SLT registration | R10 | Check relevant board or overseas register (confirm with counsel) | Number, expiry |
| BLS / neonatal resuscitation | Clinical | Sight certificate | Expiry |
| Medical indemnity | R09, L01 | Sight policy | Insurer, cover, expiry |
| Professional body (CIPM, ICAN, ANAN) | R07, R08 (preferred) | Sight membership | Number |
| Pre-employment medical and Hepatitis B status | Clinical (all staff medical) | After conditional offer, by an occupational health provider; FXMed receives fit / not fit and vaccination status only, with consent | Fit status, date |
| Driver's licence | Roles that drive | Verify with FRSC | Number, expiry |

Licence expiry dates go into the HR record on hire and are reviewed monthly; reminders go out 60 and 30 days before expiry. A clinician whose licence lapses does not see patients until it is renewed. Renewal cycles differ by council and should be confirmed with each regulator (confirm with counsel).

**Criminal records:** do not request routinely. For roles with unsupervised access to children (R10, R12, home-visit nurses) consider a police character certificate, collected only with a documented lawful basis and destroyed after the check (confirm with counsel).

## 6. Offer approval

| Situation | Approvers |
|---|---|
| Offer at or below band midpoint, standard terms | Hiring manager + Managing Director |
| Clinical role (any) | Add Medical Director |
| Offer above midpoint, within band | Managing Director with written reason (experience, scarcity, competing offer) |
| Offer above band, sign-on bonus, relocation or non-standard terms | Managing Director + Medical Director, with finance check on budget |
| Locum and sessional rates | Medical Director within the approved session rate |

The offer approval form records: role ID, candidate, band, proposed pay split (basic, housing, transport), start date, probation length, approvals with dates, and confirmation that references and credentials are clear.

### Offer letter contents

- Job title, reporting line, place of work (Lagos, home visits) and hours.
- Pay split, pay date and statutory deductions: PAYE, pension (employee 8%, employer 10% under the Pension Reform Act 2014), NHF where applicable (confirm with accountant).
- Probation period (FXMed policy, e.g. 3–6 months) and notice periods during and after probation (confirm minimums under the Labour Act with counsel; note the Act's coverage of professional staff differs from manual and clerical workers).
- Leave entitlements at or above the Labour Act minimum (confirm with counsel).
- Conditions: licence current on start date, satisfactory medical, signed confidentiality and data-protection undertaking.
- Group life insurance and any HMO cover.
- Written contract within the time required by the Labour Act after the start date (confirm with counsel); FXMed practice is to sign the contract before day one.

Contractors (T01, R10) and locums (L01) get a services agreement, not an employment offer. Counsel should check these agreements so that they reflect how the work is really done (confirm with counsel).

## 7. Fairness

- Same questions, same tasks, same scorecard for every candidate in a role.
- No questions about pregnancy, marital plans, religion, ethnicity or state of origin. Interviewers who need guidance ask the recruiter.
- Reasonable adjustments offered at every interview on request.
- Salary history is not asked; salary expectation is.
- Quarterly the recruiter reviews pass rates by stage to spot unfair drop-off.

## 8. Candidate data and retention (NDPA 2023)

FXMed is the data controller for candidate data. The privacy notice on the application form explains what we collect, why, the lawful basis, how long we keep it and how to exercise rights.

| Data | Lawful basis (confirm with counsel) | Kept for | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| Application, CV, screening notes, scorecards, interview notes | Steps at the candidate's request before a contract; legitimate interest in a fair process | Unsuccessful: 12 months after the role closes (confirm with counsel) | Deleted; anonymised stats kept |
| Talent pool (unsuccessful but promising) | Explicit consent, asked separately | 24 months, consent renewed every 12 | Deleted on expiry or withdrawal |
| References | Consent | Same as application | Deleted |
| Licence and qualification evidence | Legal obligation (regulated practice) and contract | Hired: life of employment + the period required for employee records (confirm with counsel) | Moved to employee file |
| Health data (medical fitness, vaccination status) | Explicit consent / employment obligations; sensitive data | Hired: in a restricted employee file; not hired: deleted within 30 days | Deleted |
| Criminal record check | Only where justified; sensitive data | Outcome only; certificate not kept | Deleted after verification |
| NIN | Legal obligation / contract | Store minimum (verified flag, last digits) | With employee file |

**Practical rules**

- Collect only what the stage needs: no NIN, health data or references before a decision to hire.
- Candidate files live in the HR system with access limited to the recruiter, hiring manager and panel for that role. Panel access ends when the role closes.
- No CVs in personal email, personal WhatsApp or downloads folders. Email-based applications received before the tracked form exists are moved into the HR system and deleted from inboxes.
- Requests to access, correct or delete data are answered within the NDPA time limit (confirm with counsel); log every request.
- If an applicant tracking or email tool stores data outside Nigeria, check the NDPA cross-border transfer requirements first (confirm with counsel).
- A personal data breach involving candidate data is reported to the Data Protection Officer immediately; notification to the NDPC within 72 hours where required (confirm with counsel).
- A monthly job deletes or anonymises records past their retention date; the HR Officer reviews the deletion log.
- Check whether FXMed is a data controller "of major importance" that must register with the NDPC and file annual compliance audits (confirm with counsel).
