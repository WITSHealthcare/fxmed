# FXMed Compensation and Payroll

This document sets out how FXMed pays its people: the salary structure and placeholder bands by level and department, allowances (including transport for home visits across Lagos), the rules for sales commission and the clinical-ethics guardrails on it, the monthly payroll calendar, statutory deductions and employer contributions, payslips, and how raises and promotions are decided. Every ₦ figure here is a placeholder marked "to confirm"; nothing should be paid from this document until the figures and the statutory rates have been confirmed by the clinic's accountant.

Owner: Compensation and Performance Manager (to assign), with the external accountant

---

## 1. Pay principles

1. Pay is set by **level and department**, not negotiated case by case. Offers fall inside the band.
2. **No clinician is paid per test, per supplement, per diagnosis or per programme enrolment.** Clinical pay is salary plus fixed allowances.
3. Sales commission rewards booking patients into a first consultation, never what is later recommended or sold (section 4).
4. Pay is reviewed once a year (effective 1 March) using the appraisal rating (`performance-management.md`).
5. Every salary is at least the National Minimum Wage (₦70,000 per month under the 2024 amendment) (confirm with counsel for any later change).

## 4. Sales commission

Commission is paid only to staff in the Sales role, and to ambassadors under their own agreement. Clinical staff, including any clinician who also does outreach, are not paid commission.

### What earns commission

| Event | Commission (placeholder) | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| New patient attends a paid first consultation or assessment, sourced and recorded by the rep in the financial CRM | ₦X flat per attended consultation (to confirm) | Lead source recorded before booking; patient actually attended; fee received |
| Corporate wellness contract signed (sourced by the rep) | X% of first-year contract value, paid quarterly as the client pays (to confirm) | Signed by an authorised FXMed signatory; scope set by clinical lead, not the rep |
| Outreach event that leads to attended first consultations | Same flat rate per attended consultation | As above |

Flat amounts per attended consultation are used on purpose: the rep is paid the same whether the patient later needs a single follow-up or the Elite programme.

### What never earns commission

- Lab tests or test panels, whoever orders them.
- Supplements or anything from the dispensary.
- Programme enrolments, upgrades, renewals or add-ons (Gut Repair, Hormone Balance, Thyroid Recovery, Adrenal Reset, Immune Support, Autism Support, Maternal Wellness, Elite).
- Anything recommended by a clinician after the first consultation.
- Patients who booked themselves without the rep's involvement (website, Zara) unless the rep's handover is recorded first.

### Guardrails

| Guardrail | Rule |
|---|---|
| Cap | Commission is capped at 30% of the rep's monthly gross (to confirm) so base pay stays the main income |
| Clawback | If the consultation fee is refunded within 30 days because the patient was mis-sold or misled, the commission is recovered from the next payout (written agreement in the contract) (confirm with counsel: deductions need written consent) |
| Compliance gate | A failed quarterly call/chat review (clinical claims, cure claims, pressure) suspends commission for that quarter pending review |
| Vulnerable patients | No commission on bookings for minors (including Autism Support) or for patients flagged vulnerable unless a parent/guardian or the patient booked freely and the clinical team accepted the referral |
| No influence on care | Reps may not ask clinicians to recommend tests, supplements or programmes, and clinicians may not share revenue with reps. Professional codes prohibit fee-splitting and inducements for referral (confirm with counsel for MDCN, PCN and other council rules) |
| Ambassadors | Same exclusions; ambassador rewards are per attended first consultation only |
| Audit | Quarterly: Admin checks a sample of commissioned bookings against CRM records and attendance |

Commission is calculated at the payroll cut-off for consultations attended in the previous calendar month and paid with that month's salary. It is taxable through PAYE.

## 6. Payroll calendar (monthly, Lagos time)

| Date | Step | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 1st – 15th | New starters, leavers, changes in pay or bank details logged in the HR system | Admin / HR owner |
| 18th | **Cut-off**: allowance claims, overtime, commission figures, unpaid leave, deductions | Managers, sales lead |
| 19th – 20th | Draft payroll prepared; commission and home-visit allowances checked against CRM and appointments | Payroll |
| 21st | Draft reviewed and approved (two people: preparer + approver) | Accountant + founder/Admin |
| 24th | Payment instructions sent to bank | Approver |
| 25th (or last working day before) | **Salaries paid**; payslips issued the same day | Payroll |
| Within 7 working days of payday | Pension remitted to each employee's PFA | Payroll |
| By the 10th of next month | PAYE remitted to Lagos State Internal Revenue Service | Payroll |
| Monthly (date to confirm) | NHF and NSITF (ECS) remitted | Payroll |
| Annually (by 31 Jan) | Annual PAYE returns (Form H1) filed with LIRS | Accountant |
| Annually (by 1 April) | ITF contribution paid | Accountant |

All statutory due dates to be confirmed (confirm with accountant). If the 25th is a weekend or public holiday, pay on the previous working day.

## 7. Statutory deductions and employer contributions

Rates and thresholds below are FXMed's working understanding and must be confirmed before the first payroll and whenever law changes (confirm with accountant).

| Item | Law | Employee | Employer | Base | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pension (CPS) | Pension Reform Act 2014 | 8% | 10% | Basic + housing + transport | Remitted to the employee's chosen PFA. Applies to employers with 3 or more employees. Employee picks PFA; FXMed does not |
| Group life insurance | Pension Reform Act 2014 | — | Premium | Cover at least 3× annual total emoluments | Must be in place for every employee (confirm with accountant) |
| PAYE income tax | Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), administered by LIRS for Lagos residents | Per progressive bands | — | Taxable income after reliefs | See below |
| National Housing Fund | NHF Act 1992 | 2.5% | — | Basic salary | Remitted to the Federal Mortgage Bank of Nigeria. Who must contribute and current enforcement: confirm with accountant |
| Employees' Compensation (NSITF) | Employee's Compensation Act 2010 | — | 1% | Total monthly payroll | Covers work injuries, including road accidents on home visits |
| Industrial Training Fund | ITF Act (as amended) | — | 1% | Annual payroll | Applies above a headcount/turnover threshold (5+ employees or turnover above a set amount); check current threshold (confirm with accountant) |
| Health insurance | NHIA Act 2022 | Per scheme | Per scheme | — | Whether and how FXMed must enrol staff (confirm with counsel) |

### PAYE under the current tax law

From 1 January 2026 personal income tax is under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. FXMed's working understanding of the annual bands (confirm with accountant):

| Annual taxable income | Rate |
|---|---|
| First ₦800,000 | 0% |
| Next ₦2,200,000 | 15% |
| Next ₦9,000,000 | 18% |
| Next ₦13,000,000 | 21% |
| Next ₦25,000,000 | 23% |
| Above ₦50,000,000 | 25% |

Deductible before tax (confirm with accountant): employee pension contributions, NHF contributions, NHIS/NHIA contributions, life insurance premiums, and rent relief (20% of annual rent paid, capped at ₦500,000) where the employee provides evidence of rent. The old consolidated relief allowance no longer applies. Use payroll software or the accountant's calculator, not hand calculations; re-check the bands whenever regulations or LIRS guidance change.

### Other deductions

Only deductions required by law, or agreed by the employee in writing (for example a salary advance repayment or commission clawback), are made. The Labour Act limits deductions from wages (confirm with counsel).

## 8. Payslips

Every employee receives a payslip on payday, by email as a password-protected PDF or in the HR system once built. It shows:

- Employee name, staff ID, level, department, pay period and payment date
- Basic, housing, transport and other components
- Allowances itemised (home visits listed with count and zone), commission with the count of attended consultations
- Gross pay
- Employee pension (with PFA name and RSA PIN, last 4 digits only), NHF, PAYE, other agreed deductions
- Net pay and bank (last 4 digits of account)
- Employer pension contribution (for information)
- Year-to-date gross, tax and pension

Payslips, bank details and tax IDs are personal data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: access limited to the employee, payroll and the accountant; never sent over WhatsApp; keep payroll records for at least 6 years (confirm with accountant).

Each year by 31 January, give each employee a statement of annual pay and tax deducted for their own filing.

## 9. Raises and promotion

### Annual pay review (effective 1 March)

The founder sets a total raise budget in February (for example X% of payroll, to confirm), taking account of inflation and the clinic's finances. Guideline split by appraisal rating (to confirm):

| Rating | Guideline raise | Position in band |
|---|---|---|
| 5 Outstanding | Budget × 1.5–2 | Can move above midpoint |
| 4 Strong | Budget × 1.2–1.5 | |
| 3 Meets | Budget | |
| 2 Partly meets | 0 – cost-of-living only | Not above midpoint |
| 1 Does not meet | 0 | On PIP |

Salaries do not go above the band maximum; someone at the maximum gets a one-off payment instead, or is considered for promotion. Ratings capped by the ethics override in `performance-management.md` get no merit raise that year.

### Promotion

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Time in level | Usually at least 12 months |
| Ratings | Strong (4) or above in the latest appraisal, no active PIP or final warning |
| Role need | A role at the next level exists or the current role has grown into it (written job description) |
| Licensing | Clinical promotions need a current licence and any qualification the next role requires |
| Decision | Manager proposes; founder/Admin approves at the February review (or mid-year if a role opens) |
| Pay | Move to at least the new band's minimum, typically a 10–20% increase (to confirm) |

Promotions to a role with wider portal access (for example Sales Rep to Admin) need the role changed in Users & Roles on the effective date and logged.

### Licensing and pay

Clinical staff cannot be paid for clinical work while their practising licence has lapsed. The HR system tracks each licence and its expiry; reminders at 60 and 30 days before expiry. Renewal cycles vary by council: MDCN (doctors), NMCN (nurses and midwives), PCN (pharmacists; premises licence separately), MLSCN (medical laboratory scientists) and the Dietitians Council of Nigeria (confirm the current cycle and CPD requirement for each with the council). If a licence lapses, the person moves to non-clinical duties or unpaid leave until renewed (confirm with counsel).
