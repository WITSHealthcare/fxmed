# FXMed Leave Policy

This policy sets out the leave FXMed staff are entitled to, how to request it and how managers approve it, so that people get proper rest and support while patients' home visits, consults and programmes are still covered. It applies to all employees; contractors and locums follow their own agreement. The Labour Act sets minimum entitlements; FXMed's entitlements are equal to or better than those minimums, and where this policy is more generous, the more generous terms apply. Where your contract gives more, your contract wins.

## 2. Annual leave

- **Entitlement:** 15 working days a year, rising to 20 working days after 3 years' continuous service.
- **When you can take it:** you may take accrued leave during probation for exceptional reasons only. Otherwise leave accrues from your start date and can be taken after probation.
- **Notice:** request at least **2 weeks** ahead for 1–4 days, and **4 weeks** ahead for 5 days or more.
- **Peak periods:** no more than one clinical staff member of the same profession may be on annual leave at the same time unless cover is arranged. December and Easter requests are approved first come, first served, with fair rotation year to year.
- **Carry-over:** up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year and must be used by 31 March. Anything else lapses unless FXMed cancelled your leave for business reasons.
- **Leave allowance:** where your contract includes an annual leave allowance, it is paid with the salary for the month your main annual leave starts (amount to confirm).
- **On leaving:** accrued untaken leave is paid in your final pay; leave taken beyond what has accrued is deducted (confirm with counsel).
- FXMed may ask you to take leave over a clinic closure (for example between Christmas and New Year), with at least 1 month's notice.

## 3. Sick leave

1. **Report it:** phone your supervisor before your start time, or at least 1 hour before your first booked visit. Say what is wrong (in general terms) and when you expect to return. Text messages alone are not enough unless you are unable to speak.
2. **Days 1–2:** self-certify in the HR system on your return.
3. **Day 3 onwards:** a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required. An FXMed doctor may not certify their own colleague's sickness.
4. **Pay:** full pay for up to 12 working days of sick leave in a leave year. Beyond that, the Managing Director decides case by case (for example half pay for a further period, or unpaid).
5. **Long-term or repeated absence:** after 10 working days in a row, or 3 separate absences in 3 months, your supervisor will arrange a supportive return-to-work meeting. We may ask for a medical report, with your consent, to understand what adjustments would help.
6. **Infectious illness:** do not come to work or do home visits with a fever, diarrhoea and vomiting, or any illness that could spread to patients. Clinical staff stay off for 48 hours after diarrhoea and vomiting stop.
7. **Work-related injury or illness:** report it within 24 hours; it is handled under the Employee Compensation Scheme and does not count against your sick leave (confirm with counsel).
8. Sickness during annual leave can be converted to sick leave if you report it at the time and provide a medical certificate.

## 4. Maternity leave

- **Entitlement:** 16 weeks' maternity leave at full pay (to confirm). FXMed will not provide less than the Labour Act minimum of 12 weeks or any longer period required by applicable Lagos State law (confirm with counsel).
- **Timing:** you may start leave up to 6 weeks before the expected week of childbirth. A medical certificate stating the expected date of delivery is required.
- **Notify HR:** by the end of the 5th month of pregnancy, so cover can be planned for your patients and programmes.
- **Antenatal care:** reasonable paid time off for antenatal appointments; give your supervisor the appointment date.
- **Safety in pregnancy:** your supervisor will carry out a risk assessment (lone home visits, lifting, exposure to infection or chemicals in samples) and adjust your duties.
- **Protection:** you cannot be dismissed because you are pregnant or on maternity leave (confirm with counsel). Your role is kept for you, or a suitable equivalent role on the same terms.
- **Nursing breaks:** on return, nursing mothers get two 30-minute breaks a day (or an agreed equivalent, such as a shorter day) for 6 months, and a private space to express milk (confirm with counsel).
- **Pension:** pension contributions continue on paid leave.
- **Pregnancy loss or stillbirth:** after 24 weeks, full maternity leave applies. Before that, compassionate and sick leave apply, and we will be flexible.

## 5. Paternity leave

- **Entitlement:** 10 working days at full pay, taken in one block or two blocks within 8 weeks of the birth or adoption.
- **Who qualifies:** employees whose spouse or partner gives birth, or who are adopting a child as the second carer, after completing probation.
- **Notice:** tell your supervisor by the 7th month of the pregnancy, and confirm the dates as soon as the baby arrives.
- **Evidence:** a hospital letter or birth certificate within 1 month of returning.

## 6. Compassionate and bereavement leave

| Situation | Leave | Pay |
|---|---|---|
| Death of spouse, child, parent or sibling | Up to 5 working days | Full |
| Death of grandparent, parent-in-law or other close relative | Up to 2 working days | Full |
| Serious illness or emergency of a close family member | Up to 3 working days a year | Full |
| Burial travel outside Lagos | Up to 2 extra working days | Full or annual leave, at the supervisor's discretion |

Call your supervisor as soon as you can; formal requests can be done when you return. The Managing Director may grant more in exceptional circumstances. Funeral rites and mourning customs are respected; speak to HR if you need more time.

## 7. Study and CPD leave

FXMed supports staff to keep their licences and grow. Clinical staff need CPD credits to renew their annual practising licence with MDCN, NMCN, PCN, MLSCN or the Dietitians Council.

- **Entitlement:** up to 5 working days a year of paid study leave for approved courses, conferences and CPD, plus the day of any exam that is part of an approved qualification.
- **Approval:** request at least **4 weeks** ahead with the course details, dates and cost. Your supervisor checks it is relevant to your role; the Managing Director approves any fee FXMed pays.
- **Funding:** FXMed may pay all or part of the cost. If FXMed pays more than ₦200,000 (to confirm) for a course, you sign a training bond: if you leave within 12 months of completing it, you repay a pro-rated share (confirm with counsel).
- **Record it:** upload the certificate of attendance to the HR system. It is added to your CPD record and training history.
- Mandatory FXMed training (Training tab programmes) is done in working time and is not study leave.

## 8. Public holidays

FXMed observes the federal public holidays declared by the Federal Government through the Ministry of Interior. Dates for Islamic holidays depend on the moon and are confirmed by government announcement, usually a few days ahead.

| Usual public holidays | |
|---|---|
| New Year's Day | 1 January |
| Good Friday and Easter Monday | Variable |
| Workers' Day | 1 May |
| Democracy Day | 12 June |
| Independence Day | 1 October |
| Christmas Day and Boxing Day | 25 and 26 December |
| Eid-el-Fitr (2 days), Eid-el-Kabir (2 days), Eid-el-Maulud | Variable, as declared |

- When a holiday falls on a weekend, the government usually declares the next working day a holiday; FXMed follows the declaration.
- **Holiday rota:** the clinic runs a reduced on-call service on public holidays for urgent visits and patient messages. Staff on the rota get a **day off in lieu** to be taken within 2 months, or holiday pay at the rate in their contract (rate to confirm).
- The rota is published at least 4 weeks before each holiday and shared fairly, taking religious observance into account where possible (staff are not asked to work on their own main religious festivals if it can be avoided).

## 9. Unpaid leave and other time off

- **Unpaid leave:** up to 20 working days a year by agreement, after annual leave is used. Pension contributions stop for unpaid periods (confirm with accountant).
- **Civic duties:** time off for voting, jury or court attendance as a witness, given as needed.
- **Religious observance:** reasonable flexibility for prayer times and fasting periods; agree adjustments to your visit schedule with your supervisor.
- **Medical appointments:** try to book outside working hours; if not possible, agree the time with your supervisor.

## 10. How to request and approve leave

### 10.1 Steps

| Step | Who | What | Time limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Request | Employee | Submit the request in the HR system: type, dates, number of working days, reason (for compassionate, study, unpaid), cover arranged, attachments | Notice periods above |
| 2. Check cover | Supervisor | Check the calendar for booked visits, consults and programme sessions; agree reallocation | 3 working days |
| 3. Decide | Supervisor (annual, sick, compassionate, paternity up to 10 days); Managing Director (maternity, study with fees, unpaid, anything over 10 days in a row, any refusal of a statutory entitlement) | Approve, approve with changes, or decline with a reason | 5 working days from the request |
| 4. Handover | Employee | Hand over open patients, follow-ups and leads; set an out-of-office message; note who is covering | Before leave starts |
| 5. Record | HR system | Balance updated; calendar marked; payroll notified for unpaid or part-paid leave | Automatic on approval |
| 6. Return | Employee and supervisor | Confirm return date; evidence uploaded (medical certificate, birth certificate, course certificate); return-to-work meeting after sick leave of 5+ days or any maternity leave | First day back |

### 10.2 Rules for approvers

- Decide fairly and consistently. Do not refuse statutory leave (sick, maternity) because of workload.
- If two requests clash, cover for patients comes first; then first submitted; then whoever had their last request refused.
- If you have to cancel approved leave for an emergency, FXMed reimburses any non-refundable costs on receipt.
- Supervisors' own leave is approved by the Managing Director.

### 10.3 Disputes

If you think a leave decision is unfair, talk to your supervisor first. If not resolved, raise it under the [Grievance Procedure](disciplinary-and-grievance.md).

### 10.4 Absence without approval

Leave taken without approval, or failure to return on the agreed date without contacting the clinic, is unauthorised absence. It is unpaid and may be handled under the Disciplinary Procedure.
