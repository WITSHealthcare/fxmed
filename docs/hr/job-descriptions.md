# FXMed Job Descriptions (2027 Hiring Plan)

This document holds one job description for every role in `docs/hr/hiring-plan.md`, using the same role IDs. Each JD is written so it can be pasted into the Careers tab of the admin portal (title, department, employment type, location, summary, responsibilities, requirements) and also used by the interview panel to build the scorecard in `docs/hr/recruiting-process.md`. Bands refer to the placeholder salary bands in the hiring plan and are all "to confirm". Licence requirements describe what we check; renewal cycles and registration categories must be confirmed with each regulator before the JD is published.

Owner: Head of Talent Acquisition (to assign), with each hiring manager for their role.

---

## How to read each JD

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Careers fields | Department and employment type exactly as the Careers tab expects (`Clinical`, `Operations`, `Marketing`, `Technology`, `Finance`; `Full-time`, `Part-time`, `Contract`, `Internship`, `Locum`) |
| Portal role | The admin-portal role the hire receives on day one (`admin`, `clinical`, `sales`) |
| Licences | Must be current on the start date and kept current; HR tracks expiry dates (see recruiting process, Section 6) |
| Band | Placeholder band from the hiring plan. Monthly gross in ₦, to confirm |

Every FXMed role shares these expectations, so they are not repeated in each JD:

- Follows the FXMed patient-communication rules: no cure claims, no diagnosis by non-clinicians, escalate red flags.
- Handles patient data under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and FXMed policy: no patient data in personal WhatsApp, email or photos.
- Completes the new-hire training plan (`docs/new-hire-training-plan.md`) within 90 days and the annual privacy and safety refresher.
- Works across Lagos (Island, Lekki–Ajah, Mainland) as the role requires; home-visit roles travel daily.

## R01 — Home-Visit Registered Nurse

| | |
|---|---|
| Careers fields | Clinical · Full-time · Lagos (home visits) |
| Portal role | clinical |
| Reports to | Medical Director (clinically); Clinical Operations Coordinator (schedule) |
| Band | B4: ₦350,000 – ₦550,000 a month (to confirm) |

**Purpose.** Deliver safe, warm, on-time nursing care in patients' homes for every FXMed programme, and keep the clinical record complete so the doctor can act on it.

**Responsibilities**
- Carry out scheduled home visits: vitals, anthropometry, symptom check-ins, wound and general nursing care.
- Administer prescribed injections and IV therapy under a written prescription and FXMed protocol.
- Collect blood samples when the MLS is unavailable, following the sample-handling SOP.
- Record every visit in the clinical CRM the same day; flag red flags to the doctor immediately.
- Teach patients and families about their programme (Gut Repair, Immune Support, Adrenal Reset, Elite and others) within the scope agreed by the doctor.
- Keep the home-visit kit stocked, clean and calibrated.

**Requirements**
- Registered Nurse, at least 3 years post-registration, including community, home-care or outpatient experience.
- Confident IV cannulation and phlebotomy.
- Comfortable with a tablet-based record and WhatsApp Business for patient messages (work line only).
- Able to travel across Lagos daily; driver's licence an advantage.

**Licences and certificates**
- Current NMCN registration and practising licence (confirm renewal cycle with NMCN).
- Basic Life Support (BLS) certificate, renewed every 2 years.
- Hepatitis B vaccination evidence requested at offer stage, with consent (see recruiting process).

## R02 — Medical Laboratory Scientist (Sample Collection Lead)

| | |
|---|---|
| Careers fields | Clinical · Full-time · Lagos (home visits and partner lab) |
| Portal role | clinical |
| Reports to | Medical Director |
| Band | B4: ₦350,000 – ₦550,000 a month (to confirm) |

**Purpose.** Make every FXMed lab investigation reliable from the needle to the result: correct sample, correctly handled, at the partner lab on time, and the result back in the patient's record.

**Responsibilities**
- Collect blood, urine, stool and saliva samples at home or in clinic for programme panels (thyroid, hormones, gut, micronutrients, metabolic).
- Own labelling, chain of custody, cold chain and courier hand-off to partner labs.
- Track turnaround times; chase late, rejected or haemolysed samples and arrange recollection.
- Enter or upload results so they reach the FM analysis tool and the doctor.
- Maintain the sample-handling SOP and train nurses who collect samples.
- Keep sharps, biohazard waste and spill procedures compliant.

**Requirements**
- BMLS (or equivalent), at least 2 years in a clinical lab or phlebotomy service.
- Strong paediatric phlebotomy (needed for Autism Support and newborn assessments).
- Careful with records; comfortable with spreadsheets and the portal.

**Licences and certificates**
- Current MLSCN registration and annual practising licence (confirm renewal cycle with MLSCN).
- BLS certificate.
