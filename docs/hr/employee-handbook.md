# FXMed Employee Handbook

This handbook explains how we work at FXMed: what we expect of every team member, what you can expect from us, and the rules that keep our patients, our colleagues and the clinic safe. It applies to everyone who works for FXMed in Lagos (full-time, part-time, contract, locum and interns) in the field, in virtual consults and in the office. It sits alongside your contract of employment, the [Leave Policy](leave-policy.md), the [Disciplinary and Grievance Procedure](disciplinary-and-grievance.md) and the [New-Hire Training Plan](../new-hire-training-plan.md). If this handbook and your contract disagree, your contract wins. We review the handbook every year and whenever the law or the way we work changes.

## 1. Welcome

FXMed is a functional medicine clinic. We look for the root cause of a patient's symptoms, use evidence to guide care, and bring that care to people: at home, on a video call, through lab investigations, nutrition counselling, our supplement dispensary and programmes such as Gut Repair, Hormone Balance, Thyroid Recovery, Adrenal Reset, Immune Support, Autism Support, Maternal Wellness and Elite.

We are a small team. Each person's work is visible to patients, and each person's lapse is too. In return, small means you will be heard, you will learn quickly, and you can shape how the clinic grows.

In your first week you will:

- sign your contract, this handbook acknowledgement and the confidentiality undertaking;
- hand in the documents in the table below;
- get your admin portal account (Clinical, Sales or Admin role) and start the FXMed Foundations programme in the **Training** tab;
- meet your buddy and your supervisor.

## 2. Your employment

### 2.1 Probation

New employees serve a probation period of **3 months** (6 months for senior roles, as stated in your contract). It lines up with the 30, 60 and 90-day check-ins in the training plan. Your supervisor confirms, extends (once, by up to 3 months) or ends employment at the end of probation and records the outcome in writing.

You are paid monthly, in arrears, by bank transfer on or before the **last working day of the month**. Your payslip shows gross pay, each deduction and net pay.

### 2.3 Notice

Notice periods are in your contract. They will never be shorter than the minimum in the Labour Act (confirm with counsel):

| Continuous service | Minimum notice (either side) |
|---|---|
| Up to 3 months | 1 day |
| 3 months to under 2 years | 1 week |
| 2 years to under 5 years | 2 weeks |
| 5 years or more | 1 month |

Most FXMed contracts give longer notice (usually 1 month after probation). On leaving you return all FXMed property, including devices, ID badge, uniforms, keys, clinical kit and any patient material, and your portal access is closed on your last day.

### 2.4 Professional licensing

Clinical staff must hold a current licence with their regulator for the whole of their employment and may not practise without it.

| Profession | Regulator | Renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Doctors | Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN) | Annual practising licence |
| Nurses and midwives | Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) | Licence renewal on the council's cycle (confirm with counsel) |
| Pharmacists | Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) | Annual; premises licence for the dispensary also annual |
| Medical laboratory scientists | Medical Laboratory Science Council of Nigeria (MLSCN) | Annual |
| Dietitians | Dietitians Council of Nigeria (confirm with counsel) | Annual |

- Send HR a copy of your renewed licence **before** the old one expires. HR reminds you 60 and 30 days before expiry.
- If your licence lapses, is suspended or has conditions placed on it, tell your supervisor the same day. You will be taken off patient-facing work until it is restored. Working without a valid licence is gross misconduct.
- CPD needed for renewal is supported through study leave (see the Leave Policy).

## 4. Working hours and home-visit working

### 4.1 Standard hours

| | Office / virtual | Field (home visits) |
|---|---|---|
| Core hours | Monday–Friday, 8:00–17:00 Lagos time | Scheduled visits between 7:00 and 19:00 |
| Saturdays | By rota | By rota (Maternal Wellness, Elite and urgent visits) |
| Breaks | 1 hour lunch | Taken between visits; plan it into the route |
| Weekly hours | 40 hours, as in your contract | 40 hours, as in your contract |

Overtime, Saturday and on-call work are paid or given back as time off in lieu as set out in your contract. Time off in lieu must be agreed in advance and taken within 2 months.

### 4.2 Home-visit working

- Visits are booked in the admin portal calendar. **Every visit you make must be in the calendar** with the patient's address, so the clinic knows where you are.
- Plan routes with Lagos traffic in mind. Leave enough time; if you will be more than 15 minutes late, tell the patient and the clinic.
- Check in with the clinic (WhatsApp business line or phone) when you arrive and when you leave each home. If you have not checked out within 30 minutes of the planned end, the clinic will call you (see 11.2 Lone working).
- Carry your FXMed ID badge, your visit kit and the patient's consent form if a procedure or sample is planned.
- Samples go to the lab partner the same day under the cold-chain and labelling rules in your training. Never keep samples in a personal car overnight.
- Do not take patients or their family in your car.

### 4.3 Virtual consults

- Use only FXMed-approved video tools and the FXMed account, from a private, quiet room. Use headphones.
- Confirm the patient's identity at the start and ask who else is in the room.
- No recording unless the patient has given written consent and the recording is stored in the EMR.

## 5. Attendance and punctuality

- Arrive on time for your shift or first visit. Repeated lateness is handled under the Disciplinary Procedure.
- If you cannot come in or will be late, **phone** your supervisor (not just a text) before your start time, or at least 1 hour before your first booked visit, so patients can be rebooked.
- Absence for sickness is covered in the Leave Policy. Absence without notice for 3 working days in a row may be treated as abandoning your job, after we have tried to reach you and your next of kin.
- Attendance is recorded in the HR system: office staff clock in, field staff are recorded by first visit check-in.

## 7. Code of conduct

Every team member must:

- treat patients, families and colleagues with courtesy and respect, whatever their background, tribe, religion, gender, disability, age or HIV status;
- work within your role and licence: non-clinicians do not diagnose, prescribe or interpret results; sales staff do not give clinical advice;
- follow what FXMed may and may not say: no cure claims, no guaranteed outcomes, no disparaging other providers;
- record work accurately and on time in the portal and EMR, and never alter a finalised record except through the amend function;
- handle money, stock and supplements honestly: every payment receipted, every dispensary item recorded;
- not attend work under the influence of alcohol or drugs, or take any substance that impairs your judgement;
- use FXMed devices, accounts and vehicles for work only, and keep your login to yourself;
- tell your supervisor promptly about anything that may affect your ability to do your job, including arrests or charges, licence issues or a conflict of interest.

## 8. Professional boundaries with patients

Much of our care happens in patients' homes, which makes boundaries more important, not less.

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Use FXMed's business phone and WhatsApp business line for patient contact | Give out your personal number or connect with patients on personal social media |
| Keep visits to the booked purpose and time | Run personal errands for patients, or accept hospitality beyond a glass of water |
| Have a chaperone, or offer one, for intimate examinations, and record it | Examine a patient intimately without a chaperone offer recorded in the EMR |
| Make sure a parent or guardian is present for children (Autism Support, paediatric visits) | Be alone with a child patient |
| Refer patients to FXMed services through the proper channel | Sell your own products or services to FXMed patients, or offer "private" visits outside FXMed |
| Tell your supervisor if a patient becomes over-familiar or a relationship develops | Begin any romantic or sexual relationship with a current patient, or a former patient where you could be seen to use the trust of the care relationship |

Lending or borrowing money with patients or their families is not allowed.

## 9. Confidentiality and data protection

FXMed is a data controller under the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)**. Health data is sensitive personal data and needs extra care. Every team member signs a confidentiality undertaking that continues after you leave.

Your duties:

1. **Access only what you need.** The portal limits what Clinical, Sales and Admin roles can see. Do not look up records you have no work reason to open, including your own family's or a famous patient's. Access is logged and audited.
2. **Keep data in FXMed systems.** No patient data in personal WhatsApp chats, personal email, personal cloud storage, screenshots or photos on your own phone. Use the portal, EMR and FXMed business accounts.
3. **Lock and protect devices.** Screen lock on all devices, no shared logins, log out of shared computers. Report a lost or stolen device to your supervisor and the Data Protection Officer immediately.
4. **Paper.** Keep printed results and consent forms out of sight in the car and at home; return them to the clinic and shred or scan them as instructed.
5. **Talk carefully.** Do not discuss patients in public places, on social media or with family. In the home, confirm who the patient is happy to have present before discussing results.
6. **Patients' rights.** If a patient asks to see, correct or delete their data, or withdraws consent, pass it to the Data Protection Officer the same day. Do not refuse or promise anything yourself.
7. **Report breaches at once.** Anything sent to the wrong person, a lost device, a suspicious login: tell the Data Protection Officer straight away. The NDPA requires the Nigeria Data Protection Commission to be notified within 72 hours of a qualifying breach (confirm with counsel). Reporting quickly is never a disciplinary matter; hiding a breach is.

Staff personal data held by HR (ID, bank, health, disciplinary records) is protected in the same way and is only seen by those who need it.

## 10. Social media, conflicts of interest and gifts

### 10.1 Social media

- Never post anything that could identify a patient: photos, names, home locations, lab results, voice notes or "anonymous" stories with enough detail to recognise someone. This includes WhatsApp statuses.
- Only the marketing team posts on FXMed's official accounts. You may share official FXMed posts.
- If you say you work at FXMed, make clear your views are your own. Do not give medical advice online in FXMed's name.
- Do not post about colleagues, complaints or internal matters. Raise them through the Grievance Procedure.
- Report negative or false posts about FXMed to your supervisor rather than replying.

### 10.2 Conflicts of interest

Declare in writing to HR, on joining and whenever it changes:

- other paid work, including locum shifts at other clinics, hospitals, labs or pharmacies (allowed if declared, does not clash with FXMed shifts and does not involve FXMed patients);
- any financial interest in, or close relative working at, a lab, pharmacy, supplement supplier, HMO or competitor;
- any payment, sponsorship or commission from a supplier, lab or pharma company;
- a close personal relationship with a colleague you supervise or a patient you are assigned.

Declarations are recorded in the HR system and reviewed each year. A declared conflict is usually managed simply; an undeclared one is a disciplinary matter.

### 10.3 Gifts and hospitality

| Gift | Rule |
|---|---|
| Small token from a patient (food, a card, item under ₦10,000 (to confirm)) | May accept; tell your supervisor if it happens often |
| Anything over ₦10,000 (to confirm), any cash, gift card or airtime transfer | Politely decline. If refusal would cause offence, accept on FXMed's behalf and declare it to HR within 2 working days |
| Gifts, meals, travel or "samples" from suppliers, labs or pharma | Declare all; anything over ₦10,000 (to confirm) needs Managing Director approval before you accept |
| Gifts linked to a decision (a referral, a quote, a purchase, a hire) | Never accept |

All declared gifts go in the gifts and hospitality register.

## 11. Health and safety

FXMed must provide a safe place of work, and you must work safely and report hazards. Work-related injuries and illness are covered by the Employee Compensation Scheme (NSITF); report every one to your supervisor and HR within 24 hours so a claim can be made.

### 11.1 Clinical safety

- Hand hygiene, PPE and sharps rules as in your clinical training. Sharps go in a sharps bin, never in a patient's household rubbish.
- Needlestick or splash injury: wash, report immediately, and follow the post-exposure protocol (assessment for post-exposure prophylaxis within hours).
- Hepatitis B vaccination is offered free to staff with exposure risk.
- Medicines and supplements are stored and transported as the pharmacist directs.
- Report incidents, near misses and adverse events in the incident log the same day.

### 11.2 Lone working on home visits

Most home visits are done alone. The rules:

1. **Before the visit:** the visit is in the calendar with a full address and the patient's phone number. New patients are pre-screened by phone. Any known risk (aggressive family member, dogs, unsafe area, previous incidents) is flagged on the record; such visits are done in pairs or moved to the clinic or video.
2. **Check in and out:** message the clinic's check-in line on arrival and departure.
3. **Overdue procedure:** if you have not checked out 30 minutes after the planned end, the clinic calls you. No answer after two calls in 15 minutes: the clinic calls the patient's number, then your emergency contact, then escalates to the Medical Director, who decides whether to involve security or police.
4. **Code word:** if you phone the clinic and use the agreed code word (given in training), the clinic will treat it as "I need help, call me out / send help" without the patient knowing.
5. **Your judgement wins:** if you feel unsafe at any point, leave. You will never be disciplined for ending a visit for safety reasons. Record what happened afterwards.
6. **Travel:** use the transport arrangement in your contract. Avoid late-evening visits to unfamiliar areas; visits after 19:00 need supervisor approval. Keep your phone charged; FXMed provides a power bank and data allowance for field staff.
7. **Valuables:** carry only what you need. Kit and devices are insured; your safety comes before FXMed property.

### 11.3 Office and general

- Know the fire exits, fire extinguisher and first-aid box locations.
- Report faulty equipment, electrical hazards and generator issues to Operations.
- Do not work while unwell with something infectious; see the Leave Policy for sick leave.

## 12. Anti-harassment and equal treatment

FXMed does not tolerate harassment, bullying, sexual harassment or discrimination on the grounds of sex, pregnancy, marital status, ethnicity or tribe, religion, disability, HIV status, age or any other personal characteristic. This applies to how staff treat each other, how staff treat patients, and how we protect staff from patients and their families.

- **Harassment** is unwanted conduct that violates someone's dignity or creates an intimidating, hostile or humiliating environment. Sexual harassment includes unwanted touching, comments, messages, requests for sexual favours, and any suggestion that work outcomes depend on a personal relationship.
- **Bullying** includes shouting, public humiliation, persistent unfair criticism and exclusion.
- If it happens to you, you can raise it informally (with the person, if you feel safe to) or formally through the Grievance Procedure. You may go straight to HR or the Managing Director if the issue involves your supervisor.
- If a patient or family member harasses you, end the visit if needed and report it. FXMed will act, which may include reallocating the patient, sending staff in pairs or ending the care relationship.
- Complaints are handled promptly and confidentially. Victimising anyone who complains or gives evidence in good faith is gross misconduct.

FXMed's duties under the Discrimination Against Persons with Disabilities (Prohibition) Act 2018 and the HIV and AIDS (Anti-Discrimination) Act 2014 apply (confirm with counsel). Ask HR about reasonable adjustments.

## 13. Whistleblowing

If you believe something at FXMed is wrong, unsafe, illegal or unethical, we want you to tell us. Examples: patient safety risks, a colleague practising without a licence, falsified records or results, fraud or theft, bribery, data misuse, or covering any of these up.

| Step | Who |
|---|---|
| 1. Raise it with your supervisor | If you are comfortable doing so |
| 2. Raise it with the Medical Director (clinical concerns) or Managing Director (all others) | If step 1 is not appropriate or nothing happened |
| 3. Raise it with an external body | Relevant professional council (MDCN, NMCN, PCN, MLSCN, Dietitians Council), Nigeria Data Protection Commission for data issues, or other authorities. Take advice first (confirm with counsel) |

- You can report in writing or in person and may ask for your identity to be kept confidential. Anonymous reports are accepted but are harder to investigate.
- You will get an acknowledgement within 5 working days and an update on the outcome within 30 days, as far as confidentiality allows.
- Nobody will be disciplined or treated worse for raising a genuine concern, even if it turns out to be mistaken. Retaliation is gross misconduct. Knowingly false reports made maliciously are a disciplinary matter.
