# FXMed Employee Records

This sets out what FXMed keeps on each employee's file, who may see each part, how long we keep it, and how we track professional licence expiry so no one practises on a lapsed licence. Staff files hold personal data and, for health and background checks, sensitive personal data, so they are handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) the same way we handle patient data: minimum necessary, access by role, kept no longer than needed. It applies to employees, locums, contractors, interns and NYSC corps members. Pre-start collection is in [`onboarding.md`](./onboarding.md).

Owner: People Operations Lead (to assign)

---

## 1. Principles

- **One file per person**, held in the HR system (not in email threads, WhatsApp or personal drives). Paper originals are seen, copied, stamped "original seen" and returned.
- **Purpose first.** Every item on the file has a reason (contract, payroll, statutory returns, patient safety, legal defence). If we can't say why we hold it, we don't collect it.
- **Health and background data is sensitive.** Medical fitness, immunisation status, police checks and disciplinary details sit in a restricted section that line managers can't open.
- **Employees can see their own file.** They can ask for a copy and for corrections under the NDPA (confirm with counsel the response deadline we must meet).
- **Access is logged.** Viewing or changing a staff file is recorded in the activity log, like other portal actions.
- **Portal Admin ≠ HR access.** Today the portal's Admin role sees every tab. Employee records need their own permission (People Ops, plus the MD), so that an Admin who manages the blog or careers page can't read salaries or health data.

## 2. What is on the file and who can see it

Access key: **E** = the employee, **PO** = People Ops, **MD** = Medical Director / Managing Director, **LM** = line manager / supervisor, **Fin** = accountant / payroll.

| Section | Contents | E | PO | MD | LM | Fin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Job** | Job title, department, employment type, start date, location, reporting line, buddy and supervisor, portal role, working pattern | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Title, type, dates |
| **Recruitment** | Application, CV, interview notes, offer letter, signed acceptance | ✔ (except referee comments given in confidence; confirm with counsel) | ✔ | ✔ | Interview notes they wrote | — |
| **Contract** | Signed contract or service agreement, variations, confidentiality and data-protection undertaking, handbook acknowledgement | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | — | Pay clauses |
| **Pay and statutory** | Salary and allowance split, bank details, BVN (if collected), TIN, PFA and RSA PIN, NHF number, PAYE state, payslips, pension and tax remittance references | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | — | ✔ |
| **Qualifications** | Certificates, NYSC certificate, verification results | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | — |
| **Professional licences** (clinical) | Council, registration number, licence number, issue and expiry dates, verification date and method, copy of licence, indemnity certificate | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | — |
| **Checks** (restricted) | Reference responses, guarantor form and guarantor ID, address verification, police check result, consent forms | Own consent forms; outcome | ✔ | ✔ | Outcome only ("cleared") | — |

| **Training** | Training tab progress, quiz scores, survey responses, supervisor sign-offs, CPD certificates | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | — |
| **Performance** | 30/60/90 check-ins, probation outcome letters, appraisals, improvement plans | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ (their reports) | — |
| **Leave and attendance** | Leave balance, leave taken, absences, lateness | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | ✔ | Unpaid leave only |

Patient-facing work (notes, CRM activity) is **not** copied onto the staff file. If it is needed for a performance or conduct matter, reference the record ID; don't paste patient data into HR notes.

## 3. How long we keep it

The NDPA requires that personal data is kept no longer than necessary for its purpose; it does not give a single fixed period. The periods below are FXMed's proposed schedule, based on tax, pension and limitation periods, and **all need confirmation (confirm with counsel; tax and payroll items confirm with accountant)**. At the end of the period, the record is deleted or anonymised, and the deletion is logged.

| Record | Keep for | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Unsuccessful applicants (CV, interview notes) | 12 months after the role is filled, then delete (confirm with counsel) | Respond to queries or complaints about the process; longer only with the applicant's consent to keep them on file |
| Personal details, job, contract, qualifications | Employment + 6 years (confirm with counsel) | Contract claims limitation period |
| Payroll, payslips, PAYE, pension and NHF records | Employment + 6 years, or longer if the tax authority requires (confirm with accountant) | Tax audits and pension queries |
| Professional licence and indemnity records | Employment + 6 years (confirm with counsel); clinical negligence claims may need longer, e.g. where the patient was a child (confirm with counsel) | Show that the clinician was licensed on the date of any patient contact |
| Workplace injury and Employees' Compensation records | Employment + 6 years minimum (confirm with counsel) | Compensation claims |
| Training records and sign-offs | Employment + 6 years (confirm with counsel) | Show competence at the time of care |
| Reference responses, address verification | Until probation is confirmed, then keep only "verified on [date]" | No ongoing purpose |
| Police check result | Outcome and date only; delete the certificate copy after 6 months (confirm with counsel) | Minimise sensitive data |
| Guarantor form and guarantor ID | Employment + 1 year, then delete unless a claim is open (confirm with counsel) | The guarantee's purpose ends when employment ends |
| Health questionnaire, sick notes | Employment + 2 years, unless linked to an injury or claim (confirm with counsel) | Sensitive; short purpose |
| Disciplinary warnings | Live for the period in the warning letter (e.g. 6 or 12 months), then marked spent; file kept to employment + 6 years (confirm with counsel) | Fairness to the employee; defence of claims |
| Bank details, BVN | Delete at final pay + 3 months, keeping the payroll records above | Not needed after final pay |
| Equipment register entries | Employment + 1 year | Asset reconciliation |
| Activity log entries about the employee's access | As set by the activity log policy (migration 017) | Security audit |

Legal holds: if there is a claim, dispute, council inquiry or tax audit, the MD can place a hold on the file and nothing is deleted until it is lifted.

## 4. Licence expiry tracking

A clinician practising on a lapsed licence puts patients, the clinician and FXMed at risk. Each licence is tracked as its own record, not as a note on the file.

### 4.1 What we record per licence

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Employee | Name |
| Regulator | MDCN, NMCN, PCN, MLSCN, Dietitians Council of Nigeria, other |
| Licence type | Practising licence, registration, additional qualification (e.g. RM) |
| Registration number / licence number | |
| Issue date / expiry date | |
| Verified on, verified by, method | Council portal / phone / letter |
| Evidence | Uploaded copy of the licence and the verification |
| Status | Current, expiring soon, expired, suspended, pending verification |
| Indemnity cover | Provider, policy number, expiry date |

### 4.2 Reminders and what happens at expiry

Renewal cycles differ by council and change from time to time (many are annual; confirm each with the council). We go by the **expiry date printed on the licence**, not by an assumed cycle.

| When | Action | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 90 days before expiry | Reminder to the clinician and People Ops; clinician confirms CPD credits are on track | System → clinician, PO |
| 60 days before | Reminder; clinician confirms renewal is submitted | System → clinician, PO |
| 30 days before | Reminder to clinician, supervisor and MD; status becomes **expiring soon** | System → clinician, LM, MD |
| 7 days before | Escalation to MD if no evidence of renewal uploaded | System → MD |
| On expiry, with no new licence verified | Status **expired**. Clinician is taken off the rota for clinical work and cannot take home visits or consultations until the new licence is verified. Portal role may be changed to restrict clinical tabs if needed. | PO + MD |
| New licence received | Verify with the council, upload, set new expiry, status **current** | PO |

The same reminders apply to indemnity cover. A monthly licence report (all clinical staff, status and expiry date) goes to the MD on the first working day of each month.

Locums are checked at every booking if their licence will expire before the session date.

## 5. Requests, corrections and breaches

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Employee asks for a copy of their file | People Ops provides it within the NDPA deadline (confirm with counsel), redacting third-party data such as confidential references |
| Employee asks for a correction | Correct factual errors; for disputed opinions (e.g. appraisal), add the employee's comment rather than delete |
| Reference request from a new employer | Only with the employee's consent; confirm dates, job title and (if asked) re-employment eligibility; no health or disciplinary details unless counsel advises |
| Request from police, a council or a court | Pass to the MD; release only what is lawfully required (confirm with counsel) |
| Lost laptop, wrong email, or unauthorised access to a staff file | Report to People Ops and the data protection lead the same day; assess whether it must be notified to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and to the employee within the statutory deadline (confirm with counsel) |

## Before using this

- Confirm the retention schedule with counsel and the accountant.
- Confirm whether FXMed must register with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or appoint a Data Protection Officer at its size and data volume (confirm with counsel).
- Decide who besides People Ops and the MD gets HR access, and add a separate HR permission to the portal rather than relying on the Admin role.
- Confirm each council's current renewal cycle and online verification route.
- Name the data protection lead.
