# FXMed Pre-Start and Onboarding

This is how a new team member gets from "offer accepted" to "signed off after probation". It covers everything that must be in place before they see a patient or a lead (contract, documents, licence checks, guarantor and background checks, portal accounts, equipment), what happens on day one and in week one, and the 30/60/90-day check-ins that end probation. Training content is not repeated here: it lives in [`docs/new-hire-training-plan.md`](../new-hire-training-plan.md) and is run from the **Training** tab. What we keep on file, who can see it and for how long is in [`employee-records.md`](./employee-records.md).

Owner: People Operations Lead (to assign)

---

## The timeline at a glance

| Stage | When | Done when | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Offer accepted** | Day −14 or earlier | Signed offer letter back; start date agreed | People Ops |
| **Pre-start** | Day −14 to Day −1 | Every pre-start item below is ticked, or has a written exception signed by the Medical Director / MD | People Ops + hiring manager |
| **Day one** | Day 1 | Contract signed, accounts working, buddy and supervisor met | People Ops + supervisor |
| **Week one** | Days 1–5 | Foundations programme 100% in the Training tab | Supervisor |
| **Check-ins** | Days 30, 60, 90 | Each check-in recorded on the employee file | Supervisor |
| **Probation ends** | Day 90 (or extended date) | Confirmation letter issued, or written extension / exit | People Ops + MD |

Clinical staff do **not** see patients, alone or supervised, until licence verification and indemnity are filed. Sales staff do not contact leads until the portal account and the privacy quiz (Module 1.3) are done.

## 1. Offer accepted

Send within 2 working days of the verbal yes:

- **Offer letter** with: job title, department (Clinical, Operations, Marketing, Technology, Finance), employment type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Internship, Locum), start date, work location (Lagos, mobile/home visits, remote days if any), reporting line, gross monthly pay broken into basic / housing / transport, probation length, and that the offer depends on the pre-start checks below.
- **Pre-start pack** (email + a single upload link): the document list in section 2.2, the guarantor form where it applies, the NDPA privacy notice for staff, and the bank/pension/tax details form.

Placeholder pay bands (gross per month, **to confirm** by the MD and accountant before any offer goes out):

| Role | Band (₦/month, to confirm) |
|---|---|
| Medical Officer / Physician | ₦ 450,000 – ₦ 800,000 (to confirm) |
| Registered Nurse / Midwife | ₦ 250,000 – ₦ 400,000 (to confirm) |
| Pharmacist (dispensary) | ₦ 300,000 – ₦ 500,000 (to confirm) |
| Medical Laboratory Scientist | ₦ 250,000 – ₦ 400,000 (to confirm) |
| Dietitian / Nutritionist | ₦ 220,000 – ₦ 380,000 (to confirm) |
| Sales Rep / Patient Growth | ₦ 180,000 – ₦ 300,000 + commission (to confirm) |
| Front desk / Patient coordinator | ₦ 150,000 – ₦ 250,000 (to confirm) |

Where the offer is for a role that was advertised on the careers page, mark the opening filled in **Careers** once the offer is signed.

## 2. Pre-start checklist

Work through this as one checklist per hire. Nothing is "done" until the evidence is uploaded to the employee file.

### 2.1 Contract

| Item | Notes |
|---|---|
| Written contract of employment | Prepared from the FXMed template for the employment type. The Labour Act requires written particulars of employment to be given within a set period of starting (confirm with counsel); we give them **before** day one so it is never in question. |
| Contract contents | Parties, job title and duties, start date, place of work, pay and pay dates, hours, leave, sick pay, notice periods, probation, confidentiality, patient data handling, conflict of interest, restrictive covenants (if any, confirm with counsel they are enforceable), disciplinary and grievance route, and that the staff handbook forms part of the terms. |
| Notice periods | Contract notice should be at least the Labour Act minimums for length of service (confirm with counsel). |
| Locum and contractor agreements | Separate service agreement, not an employment contract. Still requires licence check, indemnity and an NDPA data-processing clause (confirm with counsel on employment status). |
| Interns and NYSC corps members | Placement letter, supervisor named, no unsupervised patient contact. Check stipend and any PPA obligations (confirm with counsel). |
| Confidentiality and data-protection undertaking | Signed separately as well as in the contract: patient data stays in the portal and the EMR, never personal WhatsApp, screenshots or personal email. |

### 2.2 Documents to collect

| Document | Who | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Government photo ID (NIN slip/card, international passport, driver's licence or voter's card) | Everyone | Name matches certificates; NIN recorded |
| Passport photograph | Everyone | For staff ID card |
| CV and certificates (degree, diploma, WAEC/NECO where relevant) | Everyone | Originals seen; copies stamped "original seen" with date and initials |
| NYSC discharge or exemption certificate | Graduates | Original seen |
| Professional registration and current practising licence | Clinical (see 2.3) | Verified with the council, not just the certificate |
| Proof of address (utility bill or tenancy, last 3 months) | Everyone | Needed for guarantor and emergency contact |
| Bank details (account name must match ID) | Everyone | For payroll |
| BVN | Everyone | Collect only if the bank or payroll provider requires it; store as sensitive (confirm with accountant) |
| Pension Fund Administrator (PFA) and RSA PIN | Everyone in pensionable employment | New employees without an RSA open one with a PFA of their choice |
| Tax Identification Number (TIN) | Everyone | Needed for PAYE |
| NHF number | Everyone covered by NHF | Register through the employer if they have none |
| Next of kin and emergency contact | Everyone | Two contacts preferred for home-visit staff |
| Medical fitness / occupational health questionnaire | Clinical, and home-visit staff | Includes Hepatitis B immunisation status for clinical staff |
| Two references | Everyone | At least one from the most recent employer or clinical supervisor |
| Guarantor form | Where appropriate (2.4) | |
| Professional indemnity cover | Clinical | FXMed policy or personal cover; certificate filed |

### 2.3 Licence verification (clinical)

Verify with the issuing body every time, not just by looking at the certificate. Record the licence number, the date it was verified, how (council portal, phone, letter) and the **expiry date**, which feeds the licence tracker in [`employee-records.md`](./employee-records.md).

| Profession | Regulator | What to verify | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doctors | Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN) | Full registration (not provisional, unless in a supervised post) and current annual practising licence | Annual (confirm current cycle with MDCN) |
| Nurses and midwives | Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) | Registration and current licence for each qualification held (RN, RM, etc.) | Confirm current cycle with NMCN; track the actual expiry date on the licence |
| Pharmacists and pharmacy technicians | Pharmacists Council of Nigeria (PCN) | Registration and current annual licence; superintendent pharmacist named if the dispensary is premises-licensed | Annual (confirm with PCN) |
| Medical laboratory scientists and technicians | Medical Laboratory Science Council of Nigeria (MLSCN) | Registration and current practising licence | Annual (confirm with MLSCN) |
| Dietitians | Dietitians Council of Nigeria | Registration and current licence; "nutritionist" titles without council registration do not do clinical nutrition counselling alone | Confirm cycle with the council |
| Physiotherapists, others | Relevant board | As above | As set by the board |

If a licence can't be verified before day one, the person can start Foundations training but does **no** clinical work until it is. Note this in writing on the file.

### 2.4 Guarantor and background checks

These are common in Lagos and we use them where the role justifies it, not by default. Every check needs the candidate's written consent and a stated purpose under the NDPA 2023, and results are kept only as long as needed (see employee-records.md).

| Check | Applies to | How |
|---|---|---|
| References | Everyone | Phone or email the referee; record who, when, and the answer to "would you re-employ?" |
| Certificate verification | Everyone with a qualification the role depends on | Institution or council confirmation |
| Previous clinical conduct | Clinical | Ask the council if there are sanctions; ask the last clinical supervisor directly |
| Guarantor | Roles that handle cash, stock (dispensary, supplements), company equipment, drivers, and staff visiting patients' homes | Guarantor form with ID and address; guarantor is a working adult, not a co-worker; call the guarantor to confirm they signed. Scope and enforceability of the guarantee (confirm with counsel) |
| Address verification | Home-visit and dispensary staff | Visit or third-party verifier |
| Police character / criminal record check | Staff working alone with children (Autism Support), in patients' homes, or with controlled stock | Through the Nigeria Police or an accredited provider; consent first (confirm with counsel what may lawfully be requested and kept) |

A failed or adverse check goes to the MD before any decision. Do not tell the candidate the reason by phone; put it in writing.

### 2.5 Statutory registrations

Set up before the first payroll. The figures here are what we currently understand; the accountant confirms each one before payroll runs.

| Scheme | What we do | Rate / note |
|---|---|---|
| Pension (Pension Reform Act 2014) | Add to monthly pension schedule with their PFA and RSA PIN | Employee 8%, employer 10% of monthly basic + housing + transport (confirm with accountant); remit within the statutory deadline after payday (confirm with accountant) |
| Group life insurance | Add the new hire to the policy | Required under the Pension Reform Act (confirm cover level with accountant/broker) |
| PAYE | Add to payroll with TIN; tax resident state is where they live (most staff: Lagos State, LIRS) | Calculated under the current personal income tax law and bands (confirm with accountant, as the rules changed recently) |
| NHF | Deduct and remit for eligible staff | Percentage of basic salary as set by the NHF Act (confirm with accountant) |
| NSITF / Employees' Compensation Scheme | Include in the monthly payroll figure | Employer contribution as a percentage of payroll (confirm with accountant) |
| ITF | Include in the annual payroll figure | Applies once FXMed meets the headcount or turnover threshold (confirm with accountant) |
| HMO / health cover for staff | Enrol if we offer it | Policy-dependent |

### 2.6 IT and portal accounts

Set up by an Admin no earlier than 3 working days before the start date, so accounts aren't sitting unused.

| Account | Notes |
|---|---|
| FXMed email (name@...) | Work email only; used for the portal login |
| Admin portal account | Created in **Users** with the right role, nothing more: **Clinical Team Member** for clinicians (clinical CRM view, EMR, FM Analysis), **Sales Rep** for sales (financial CRM view, messages, SEO), **Admin** only for people who manage staff, careers and settings. Admin is never given "just in case". Supervisor confirms the role in writing. |
| Training assignments | Assign **FXMed Foundations** plus the role track in the Training tab before day one, so it shows on their first login |
| Work phone / WhatsApp Business line | Patient messages go through the work line only |
| Shared calendars | Appointment calendar access as per role |
| Lab and supplier portals | Only for staff who order (clinical, dispensary) |
| Password manager and 2-step verification | Set up on day one with IT |

No shared logins. Every account belongs to one named person so the activity log means something. On exit, the Admin turns off dashboard access in **Users** on the last working day (see the Platform Admin module in the training plan).

### 2.7 Equipment

| Item | Who | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Laptop or tablet | Everyone who uses the portal daily | Encrypted, screen lock, asset tag; signed out on an equipment form |
| Work phone / SIM | Sales, coordinators, home-visit clinicians | Asset tag; no personal patient chats on personal phones |
| Home-visit kit | Home-visit clinicians | BP monitor, glucometer, pulse oximeter, thermometer, phlebotomy supplies, sharps box, PPE, cool box for samples; checked against the kit list in Module 2.1 |
| Uniform / scrubs and staff ID card | Everyone patient-facing | ID card shows name, role and photo, not personal phone |
| Transport arrangement | Home-visit staff | Ride allowance or pool vehicle; agreed in writing |

Every item goes on the equipment register for the employee and comes back on exit.

## 4. Week one

Week one is the **FXMed Foundations** programme. Follow [`docs/new-hire-training-plan.md`](../new-hire-training-plan.md) (Programme 1), tracked in the Training tab. Alongside it, People Ops makes sure:

- Every pre-start item is closed, or has a dated exception approved by the MD.
- Payroll, pension, PAYE and NHF details are with the accountant before the payroll cut-off.
- The new hire has read and acknowledged the staff handbook (leave, sick reporting, conduct, social media, gifts from patients).
- The supervisor holds the **end of week one check-in** (training plan, "Check-ins and sign-off") and records it.

## 5. 30/60/90 check-ins

The training plan sets the training targets for each check-in. The check-ins below add the employment side: conduct, attendance, fit with the team, and whether probation is on track. Each check-in is a 30-minute conversation, written up the same day and signed by both.

| When | Who | Training (from training plan) | Employment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | Supervisor | Role track complete; supervised-practice targets agreed | Attendance and punctuality; any conduct or patient-feedback issues; is the role what they expected; wellbeing |
| Day 60 | Supervisor | Supervised work reviewed | On track for confirmation? If not, say so now, in writing, with what needs to change and by when |
| Day 90 | Supervisor + Admin + MD | Independent-practice sign-off | Probation decision (section 6) |

Rate each area **Meets / Partly meets / Doesn't yet meet** with a sentence of evidence. "Doesn't yet meet" at day 60 must come with a written improvement plan.

## 6. Probation

| Item | FXMed practice |
|---|---|
| Length | 3 months (90 days) from start, matching the training plan. The Labour Act does not set a probation period, so the length and terms come from the contract (confirm with counsel). |
| Extension | Once, by up to 3 months, in writing before the original end date, with reasons and targets. |
| Notice during probation | As stated in the contract, not less than the statutory minimum (confirm with counsel). |
| Outcome letters | **Confirmation** letter on passing; **extension** letter with targets; or **termination** letter with notice. Templates to be reviewed by counsel. |
| Clinical staff | Cannot be confirmed without the supervisor's "Ready for independent practice: Yes" sign-off (Module 2.6) and a current licence on file. |
| Fairness | Concerns raised at day 30 or 60 are written down and shared with the employee. No one should hear about a probation problem for the first time at day 90. |
| Benefits during probation | Which benefits start on day one and which on confirmation (leave accrual, HMO, bonus eligibility) is stated in the offer letter. Pension, PAYE, NHF and other statutory items apply from the start (confirm with accountant). |

After confirmation, the new hire moves to the ongoing cycle in the training plan (annual privacy refresher, quarterly case/call review) and the normal appraisal cycle.
