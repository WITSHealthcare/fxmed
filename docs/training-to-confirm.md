# New-hire training: what FXMed needs to confirm

The 10 training programmes from the [new-hire training plan](new-hire-training-plan.md) are in the admin portal's **Training** tab as drafts. Staff can't see drafts. The lessons were written without guessing anything only the clinic knows: wherever a fact was missing, the lesson says **[to confirm: …]** instead. There are 238 of these across 114 lessons.

Many of them repeat one decision (the fee schedule, the data protection lead, emergency numbers). So this checklist has three parts:

1. **Decisions to make (Part A):** about 60 answers, grouped by who owns them. One answer usually fills several lessons.
2. **Problems the writers found (Part B):** things in the portal, website or policies that don't match, and that the lessons work around.
3. **Every placeholder, lesson by lesson (Part C):** where to paste each answer in the Training tab.

**How to use it:** answer Part A first, then go through Part C in the Training tab (Training → open the programme → edit the lesson) and replace each `[to confirm: …]` with the answer. Publish a programme only when its Part C list is clear and, for clinical programmes, the Medical Director has signed it off.

Programmes: **P1** FXMed Foundations · **P2** Clinical Team Track · **P3** Sales & Patient Growth Track · **4.1** Medical Director · **4.2** Physician · **4.3** Nutritionist · **4.4** Care Navigator · **4.5** Business Development Manager · **4.6** Digital Media Technician · **PA** Platform Admin · **SA** Stomach Acid & Digestion

Clinical courses added later (not counted in the totals above): **SA** Stomach Acid & Digestion · **FND** Foundations of Functional Medicine · **NBG** When Patients Don't Get Better · **GUT** Gut Health, Food Sensitivities & the Gallbladder · **NUT** Nutrition, Diet Change & Supplements · **LAB** Everyday Lab Work & Iron · **TOX** Toxic Load, Biotransformation & Methylation · **HOR** Hormone Health · **MET** Metabolic Dysfunction & GLP-1 Medicines · **IMM** Immune & Nervous System Balance. Lessons note where common functional medicine teaching differs from clinical guidelines; the Medical Director should check those notes as part of sign-off.

## Part B — Problems the writers found

These are in the portal, the website or the policies. The lessons work around them, but each needs a decision or a fix.

**Portal**
1. **Admins can't complete training.** Admin accounts only see the course builder, not the learner view, so the Medical Director and Platform Admin programmes can't be completed in the portal.
2. **The hands-on booking exercise (P1 1.4) uses the live public booking form.** It creates a real contact and opens the Paystack payment page. The lesson says to close the payment page without paying.
3. **Access gaps:**
   - Sales can't open the patient-stage CRM, the "+ New Lead" form, the Zara tab, the Ambassador Program tab or the Blog tab.
   - Clinical has no Messages tab.
   - Messages can't send a reply; staff reply by email, WhatsApp or phone.
4. **Every Clinical account can prescribe and finalise notes,** including Nutritionists and Care Navigators. The lessons say plainly that the portal allowing something doesn't make it part of the role.
5. **There's no automatic allergy or interaction check when prescribing.**
6. **Finalised notes can still be edited** (each edit is audited). The lessons teach "Add amendment" instead.
7. **Users & Roles creates accounts with a temporary password** that the Admin passes on. There's no email invite, and no control for changing a user's role.
8. **There's no incident log** in the portal, although the handbook refers to one.
9. **Meal Plan Designer:** it sends the uploaded file to an external AI service, and it keeps its history in the browser on that device, not in the patient record.
10. **SEO Analytics shows no live data,** and the CRM Scenarios figures (₦77M / ₦65M / ₦40M) are hard-coded.
11. **"Publish Now" is the main button on a new blog post** and goes live straight away.

**Zara (website assistant)**

12. Zara attaches the ₦350,000 base fee to the lab panel and meal plan. The website's Pricing section shows it only on the Corporate / Outreach packages.
13. Zara has no emergency instruction, and is told never to say it's an AI assistant.

**Website wording**

14. Public programme pages make claim-like statements, for example "heal your digestive system", "restore thyroid function naturally", "no prescription drugs with side effects" and "chronic disease-free life". The lessons tell staff not to repeat them, and that "adrenal fatigue" is not a recognised diagnosis.

**Policies that conflict**

15. **Ambassador commission:** a percentage of the membership fee (Ambassadors page and portal) or per attended first consultation (compensation-and-payroll.md).
16. **Supervised practice:** 5 observed consultations (training plan) or 10 supervised cases (performance-management.md).

**Training plan**

17. Module 2.6 (supervised practice) has hours attached, so the Clinical Team Track shows 37 hours instead of the plan's ~20. Lower those lessons' minutes if you want the total to read ~20.
