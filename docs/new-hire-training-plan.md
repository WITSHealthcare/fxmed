# FXMed New-Hire Training Plan

This plan takes a new team member from day one to working on their own within 90 days. It is built to load straight into the **Training** tab of the admin portal: each programme below is a training programme, each module is a module, and each lesson has a duration and, where marked, a quiz (graded) or survey (feedback).

There are three shared programmes, plus a short role programme for each position on the website:

| Programme | Audience role | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 1. FXMed Foundations | Clinical and Sales | Week 1 (≈ 12 hours) |
| 2. Clinical Team Track | Clinical | Weeks 2–4 (≈ 20 hours) + supervised practice |
| 3. Sales & Patient Growth Track | Sales | Weeks 2–4 (≈ 16 hours) + shadowing |
| 4. Role programmes (4.1–4.6) | Assigned by member email | Weeks 2–6 (≈ 3–6 hours each) |

Admins take Foundations plus whichever track matches their day-to-day work, and the Platform Admin module at the end.

## How the 90 days run

| Period | Focus | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** | Foundations: who we are, how we care for patients, data protection, the admin portal | Foundations programme 100%, all quizzes passed |
| **Weeks 2–4** | Role track lessons, shadowing an experienced colleague | Role track 100%, shadowing log signed off |
| **Days 30–60** | Supervised work: the new hire does the work, a supervisor reviews it | 30-day check-in held; supervisor has reviewed the agreed number of cases or leads |
| **Days 60–90** | Independent work with spot checks | 60- and 90-day check-ins held; sign-off recorded |

Each new hire has a named **buddy** (a peer for day-to-day questions) and a **supervisor** (signs off competence). Both are named on day one.

Track completion in the Training tab (progress %, quiz scores and survey responses per learner).

## Ongoing (after 90 days)

- **Every year:** privacy and safety refresher (Module 1.3) and quiz.
- **Whenever reference ranges or protocols change:** a short "what changed" lesson for clinical staff, with a quiz.
- **Quarterly:** one case review or call review per person.

## Before rolling this out

Someone at FXMed needs to fill in what only the clinic knows:

- Named owners for each module, and who acts as buddy and supervisor.
- Current fee schedule and package rules (Module 1.5, 3.1).
- Emergency, safeguarding and escalation contacts (Module 1.3).
- Clinical sign-off of the programme protocols in Module 2.4 and of the FM reference ranges.
- Confirmation of the data protection lesson by whoever handles compliance.
- The portal role for the Care Navigator: `clinical` gives the EMR but not the Messages tab, and `sales` gives Messages but not the EMR (and shows the financial CRM). They may need a new role. and blog access for the Digital Media Technician (the `sales` role has no blog tab today).
- Job descriptions for the website positions with no matching JD: Care Navigator, Business Development Manager and Digital Media Technician. Also whether "Nutritionist" on the website should read "Dietitian" for council-registered staff.
